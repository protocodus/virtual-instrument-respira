# Fix pass, 2026-11-10

A second reading of the model after the 2026-11-09 consolidation
([audit](consolidation-audit-2026-11-09.md)), on the half rate (0.1.25).
Defects only: no control, mechanism or preset was added and nothing was
re-voiced. What was measured for the half rate itself is in
[`Docs/benchmarks/half-rate-2026-11-10.md`](../benchmarks/half-rate-2026-11-10.md).

## Fixed

### 0.1.25: low host rates (with the half rate)

`Respira.DspFuzzReplay` aborted on the half-rate engine: at an 8 kHz host the
output went non-finite. The chamber's pressure and the pallet hole's flow
step in turn, which holds only while the step is shorter than 1/π of their
resonance's period, and the top chambers resonate from 5.9 kHz. On two steps
per sample 844 of 1360 key renders went non-finite at 8 kHz and 33 (A♯7–C8)
at 11.025 kHz. 0.1.24 had the same fault lower down: `Prepare` accepted any
rate above 1 kHz, and at 2–6 kHz hosts 28 to 1360 renders went non-finite.
Now a host rate under 40 kHz keeps four steps per sample and one under 8 kHz
is taken for 48 kHz. Test: `LowHostRatesHold` (8, 11.025, 16, 22.05 and
32 kHz finite; 4 kHz plays A4 as 48 kHz does), which fails on 0.1.24 at
4 kHz.

### 0.1.26: a folded key cut the note of the key it shares a reed with

Treble keys outside C2–C8 fold by octaves onto the reeds inside
(`TrebleKey`), so C♯8 opens C♯7's chamber. `NoteOff` looked for another held
key on the same reed only on the bass side; on the treble side the chamber
went to the later key and shut with it. With C♯7 held, pressing and releasing
C♯8 left C♯7 silent under its finger. Now the reed passes back to a key
still down, as a bass button's does. Test: `FoldedKeysShareAReed`, whose
first case fails on 0.1.25 (no cell left open).

### 0.1.26: the air meter's smoothing was a count of samples

`Diag().airFlow` smoothed the reeds' pulsing flow by 0.002 a sample at every
rate: 10 ms at 48 kHz, 5 ms at 96 kHz, where a low reed's meter rippled 1.75
times as far (Bassoon Low, C3). Now 10 ms at every rate, the same to the bit
at 48 kHz. The meter is the only reader; no sound changes. Test:
`AirMeterKeepsItsTimeAtEveryRate`, which fails on 0.1.25 (ratio 1.75).

### The budget test's stress phrase held the air button

Found on 2026-11-09 and left to the shared renderer's owner: the portfolio's
default stress phrase rings two chords under CC 64, which is Respira's air
button, so the sixteen keys played into vented bellows (6–14 Pa, −50 dBFS)
and the reeds hardly sounded. The shared renderer takes a phrase per
instrument (`Instrument::stress`), so Respira now gives its own
(`Tools/StressPhrase.h`): the same eight keys held, each struck again, the
bellows turned by keyswitch, a second chord a tone higher on top and a second
turn under all sixteen keys, on Auto as a loaded preset plays. Through Master
Tutti it fills all 72 cells at 350–450 Pa and −19 dBFS. Test:
`StressPhrasePlaysTheReeds`. No shared file was edited.

## Stale text fixed

- The README's Known gaps and `ReedBank.h` gave the top reeds' starting
  pressures as 100 Pa at C♯7, 140 at D7 and 265–280 at C8. Those were
  0.1.14's; 0.1.20 lowered the reeds' set. Re-measured: 80, 85 and 220 Pa on
  the half rate (75, 85 and 215 on 0.1.24).
- The README and `ReedModel.h` said the chamber's duct has sections "of at
  most 5 mm". The rule gives as many sections as fit at 5 mm or more, and at
  least 1.46 steps of sound's travel: both now say so.
- `RespiraControls.h`, `RespiraEngine.h`, `ReedModel.h`, `CompareAttacks.cpp`
  and the README's How it works named 192 kHz or four times the host rate.

## Found and not fixed

- **A few bass tongues' pitch depends on the step rate**, and the tuner
  leaves 1.3 cents on one tongue at its own rate (the benchmark document's
  tuning table). At the default setting every tongue is within 1.3 cents;
  the 5.4 cents is at Oversampling 2×. Taking it out needs a table per step
  rate or a tuner that follows the played rate, a design change, not a fix.
  The README's Known gaps now carry it.
- **The chamber grid differs between a 44.1 and a 48 kHz host** since the
  half rate (193 of 413 chambers, one section). Tuning holds within 0.13
  cents at both and the demos differ by at most 1.1 dB in the top octave,
  against 0.4 dB in 0.1.24. A rule that keeps one grid at both rates either
  cuts the 44.1 kHz step's stability margin or changes the 48 kHz sound the
  owner chose, so it was left and recorded.
- **Auto's strokes differ between host rates.** In `RespiraKeyboardSurvey` a
  "400 Pa" stroke measures 380 Pa at 48 kHz and 444 Pa at 44.1 kHz, in 0.1.24
  as in 0.1.25. That is each stroke's own level (`NewStroke`, ±10 %): it is
  drawn from a generator that advances every sample, so the same phrase gets
  other strokes at another rate. By design ("no two strokes alike"); it
  makes rate comparisons under Auto meaningless, and the survey's protocol
  says so.
- **The committed demo WAVs** (`Docs/audio/*.wav`) date from `f9057b3`, long
  before the half rate. They come from the main build, which Respira does not
  have (Known gaps, No plug-in yet); CI uploads fresh renders as an artifact.
- **The 10 % ceiling**: four demos now stay under it, four and the stress
  phrase do not (README, Known gaps).
