# Physical-model review, 0.1.11

Scope: the model as of `0023d2e` (0.1.10), read against its own documents and
its tools. Nothing here changes a reed equation, a calibrated constant or a
control; each change below is a defect fix with a test, found by reading and by
measuring, not by ear. **No listening verdict exists for any of them.**

## Fixed (each its own commit, with a test)

| Finding | Evidence | Test |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The bass-strap knock took `hand.velocity * 0.15`, with the hand's speed in wheel ranges a second, as m/s. It scaled with nothing physical and ignored Bellows. | At the default Bellows a 1 range/s turn knocked at 0.15 m/s; the bass end moves at litres × velocity ÷ (0.41 × 0.19 m²), 0.026 m/s at the default 2 L. README statements corrected to the tools' current output: the harmonic
agreement against the published accordion (`CompareHarmonics`), the
bandoneon pitch slopes (`CompareBandoneon`), the bent-pallet figures, the Room
mode spacing, the strap paragraph (it was "silent" while 0.1.10 knocked it) and
the pull valves (it said their slaps are not heard while 0.1.10 models them).
The ledger gained entries for 0.1.9 and 0.1.10, which had none.

## One test limit changed

`BlockPartitionInvariance` bounded the difference between block sizes by 1e-20,
the silent-callback flush floor. With the tremor at its intended size the
4 096-frame run differed from the 1-frame run by 1.36e-20 at one sample of
1.66e-13, one float ulp: a float sample that small has an ulp of 1e-19, above
the floor, so the flush can move it by one ulp. The check now allows one ulp
where a sample is under 1e-12 (−240 dBFS) and holds every louder sample to the
unchanged floor. Reason is beside the code.

## Looked at and not changed

- **Octave bass dropout beyond two buttons** is handled by the same change (the
  chamber moves to any held button of the group and pitch class).
- **Reed second mode, a better half-band decimator, soft-start physics, a
  finer 14-bit wheel step.** Each was considered; no measurement here shows a
  defect the change would remove, so none was made. The decimator's alias level
  and the low-pressure start remain as the README's Known gaps describe them.
- **Skipping unexcited reeds** was prototyped and gave no speed-up
  ([CPU note](../benchmarks/folds-cpu-2026-11-03.md#tried-and-not-kept)).

## Not re-measured

The README's other Known-gaps figures (the 2nd-harmonic mechanism at A3, the
Okada comparison, bend per kPa, the top-octave comparisons) were written
against 0.1.8 and the reed changes since then may have moved them; only the
numbers the shipped tools print directly were refreshed. Treat the rest as
0.1.8 figures.

## Version

The branch carries 0.1.11 for all four changes: it is unreleased and no
intermediate version was handed out.
