# Eco: the reeds at the host rate

0.5.0 adds **Eco** to Oversampling: the reed cells step once per host sample
where 1× steps them twice. These are the figures behind the README's entry,
1× against Eco in the same build, at 48 kHz.

## How it was measured

Intel Xeon @ 2.10 GHz, a shared cloud container; GCC 13.3, `-O3`, plain
x86-64. Development-machine figures, never Reason's or a DAW's. A harness
plays a demo phrase or the stress phrase through its preset as
`RenderRespiraPresetDemos` does, five times, and keeps every 256-frame
block's shortest time; a block's share is its time over 256 / 48000 s. The
sound is the same harness's float output.

## CPU

| Render | 1×, mean | 1×, worst block | Eco, mean | Eco, worst block | Mean saved |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Master Tutti demo | 12.8 % | 33.3 % | 7.2 % | 17.8 % | 44 % |
| Paris Musette demo | 4.2 % | 9.4 % | 2.8 % | 7.5 % | 35 % |
| Clarinet Solo demo | 2.1 % | 7.3 % | 1.8 % | 7.0 % | 14 % |
| Stress phrase, Master Tutti | 19.6 % | 51.2 % | 10.7 % | 32.0 % | 46 % |

The saving follows the number of sounding reeds: the bellows, the room, the
mechanism and the microphones do not step with them.

(`OversamplingKeepsTheSound`, `TopOctaveKeepsItsPitchAtEveryStepRate`); the
takes do not null, because the reeds' turbulence is drawn once per step.

## Why the top falls

A chamber section cannot be shorter than sound travels in a step and stay
stable (`MakeChamber`, 500 m/s over the step rate): 5.2 mm at 96 kHz, where
the sections are 5 mm, and 10.4 mm at 48 kHz. The longer sections lose the
chambers' modes above about 8 kHz. Sections of 10 mm at the 1× rate lose the
same octave (0.8–1.1 dB at 4–8 kHz, 7–9 dB at 8–12 kHz) for 13 % of the work,
so the step rate is the saving worth having.

## What was tried and left out

- Skipping a cell's idle reed while it is exactly at rest is bit-identical
  and saves nothing: the idle tongue rings with its chamber and is at rest
  for 2 % of its steps.
- Not stepping the idle reed at all saves 8 % and moves the octave bands by
  0.5–1.5 dB, 3 dB at 8 kHz.
- One section per chamber saves 19 % and removes the chambers' modes.

## Where Eco applies

Only where the halved step stays at 32 kHz or faster, the slowest step the
smallest chambers are stable on (`LowHostRatesHold`): one step a sample from
a 40 kHz host rate up, two from 16 kHz to under 40 kHz (where 1× takes
four), and no change below.
