# The half rate: what 0.1.25 costs and what it changed

0.1.25 steps the reed cells twice per host sample where 0.1.24 stepped them
four times (the owner's choice by ear, `Docs/decisions.md`, 2026-11-10).
These are the figures behind the README's entry, 0.1.24 (`519e9ad`) against
0.1.25, both at 48 kHz and Oversampling 1×.

## How it was measured

Intel Xeon @ 2.10 GHz, 4 threads, a cloud container shared with five other
sessions (load 9–15 throughout); GCC 13.3, Release (`-O3`), plain x86-64.
Development-machine figures, never Reason's or a DAW's.

- **Instruction counts** (`valgrind --tool=callgrind`, `Ir`) decide: they do
  not move with the machine's load. Each is one whole render by a harness
  that plays a demo phrase through its preset as
  `RenderRespiraPresetDemos` does.
- **Block times** come from the same harness: every 256-frame block's
  shortest time over nine renders (three for 0.1.24, whose worst blocks on
  the loaded machine are therefore not given where a neighbour's work fell
  into them). A block's share is its time over 256 / 48000 s.
- **Sound**: the same harness's float output, `RespiraKeyboardSurvey`
  (every key, three pressures), and `ReedProbe.h`'s single-cell probes.

## Sound

- **The demos.** Whole-file RMS within 0.05 dB of 0.1.24 on all eight.
  Octave bands (Welch, both channels' mean): under 8 kHz within 0.2 dB
  (Master Tutti's 4–8 kHz −0.27 dB); 8–12 kHz +0.1 to +0.8 dB, 12–16 kHz +0.4
  to +1.1 dB, 16–20 kHz −0.5 to +1.5 dB. Sample peaks move with the phases
  of the top partials: Clarinet Solo +0.9 dB, Master Tutti +1.8 dB (to
  −1.8 dBFS), the rest within 0.5 dB.
- **Every key** (`RespiraKeyboardSurvey`, Clarinet Solo, Auto at about
  380 Pa): level −0.23 to +0.38 dB, pitch −0.15 to +0.03 cents, onset −6 to
  +31 ms, the pressure a key speaks at −6 to +16 Pa. At 190 Pa the largest
  level change is −1.1 dB (A♯7) and B7 joins C8 in staying silent; at 95 Pa
  the same eight keys from F7 up stay silent in both. C8 speaks at 243 Pa
  where it did at 240. Chords: within 0.18 dB and 0.13 cents.
- **The bass side**: level within 0.6 dB (1.7 dB at D1 pulled at 200 Pa);
  pitch moves by up to 3.5 cents at 400 Pa (6.9 at 100 Pa) through the remade
  table, below.
- **A top reed alone** (the pressure at which it comes within 20 dB of its
  400 Pa level after 2 s, Life 0, 5 Pa steps), 192 kHz steps against 96 kHz:
  C7 70 and 70 Pa, C♯7 75 and 80, D7 85 and 85, F♯7 115 and 120, G♯7 140 and
  145, A♯7 170 and 175, B7 195 and 200, C8 215 and 220, the same in each 8′
  rank and the 4′; the 16′ rank's reeds an octave down 40–70 in both.
- **Air** (`ProbeReed`, 400 Pa): unchanged to 0.001 L/s on every reed probed
  (C7 0.036 to 0.035 L/s).

## Tuning

`ReedTuning.h` is made at 96 kHz steps (`kTuningRate`). Each tongue alone at
400 Pa, as the engine builds it, cents from its tuning:

| Steps | Treble ranks, worst | Bass and chord ranks, worst |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 96 kHz (48 kHz host) | 0.13 | 0.24, and −1.31 on the 16′ bass D pushed |
| 88.2 kHz (44.1 kHz host) | 0.13 | 0.25, and −1.31 on the same tongue |
| 192 kHz (Oversampling 2×) | 0.17, and +0.75 on one low-tuned 8′ reed (A7 pushed) | −1.29 on that D, +5.39 on the 8′ bass A♯ pulled |
| 0.1.24, 192 kHz with its own table | 0.13 | −1.25 (16′ bass C♯ pulled), −2.21 (8′ bass G pulled) |

A few bass tongues settle a few cents apart at different step rates (the
8′ bass A♯'s pull tongue, in the same nine-section chamber, by 5.4 cents
between 96 and 192 kHz), and the tuner leaves up to 1.3 cents on one tongue
at its own rate. Neither is new, and neither was changed here; the README's
Known gaps carry it.

The chamber's sections are at least 5 mm and at least 1.46 steps of sound's
travel long, so at 96 kHz steps they are 5.2 mm or more and at 88.2 kHz
5.7 mm: 105 of the 413 chambers have one section fewer than in 0.1.24 at a
48 kHz host, and 193 differ between a 44.1 and a 48 kHz host, where none did.
Measured on three demos played at both host rates (Clarinet Solo, Paris
Musette, Master Tutti), a 44.1 kHz host differs from a 48 kHz one by at most
0.6 dB in any octave band under 12 kHz and 1.1 dB in 16–20 kHz; in 0.1.24 by
0.5 and 0.4 dB.

## Low host rates

Non-finite key renders of 1360 (every key of the survey) per host rate:

| Host rate | 0.1.24 | Two steps per sample at every rate | 0.1.25 |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 2000 Hz | 1147 | | taken for 48 kHz |
| 4000 Hz | 1360 | | taken for 48 kHz |
| 5512.5 Hz | 34 | | taken for 48 kHz |
| 6000 Hz | 28 | | taken for 48 kHz |
| 8000 Hz | 0 | 844 | 0 |
| 11025 Hz | 0 | 33 (A♯7–C8) | 0 |
| 16000, 22050, 32000 Hz | 0 | 0 | 0 |

The chamber's pressure and the pallet hole's flow step in turn, which holds
only while the step is shorter than 1/π of their resonance's period, and the
top chambers resonate from 5.9 kHz. So 0.1.25 keeps four steps per sample
under a 40 kHz host rate and takes a rate under 8 kHz for 48 kHz
(`LowHostRatesHold`; `Respira.DspFuzzReplay` prepares at 8 kHz).
