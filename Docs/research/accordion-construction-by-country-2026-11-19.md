# Accordion construction by country (2026-11-19)

What differs between the kinds of accordion Respira's Type control builds,
and what each source supports. Most detail comes from makers, restorers and
tuners, not measurement; the measured studies are marked.

## Summary

National sounds differ mostly in which reed sets sound, how the tremolo reeds
are tuned and which reeds sit in a cassotto. Only the bandoneon is built
differently in kind. Seven geometrically identical reed blocks in different
materials sounded "surprisingly" alike, within about 1 dB except where the
surface texture differed, and the housing does not radiate significantly
(measured: Pomberger, Frank, Trabesinger, Höldrich, *Material-Dependent Sound
and Vibration of a Harmonica Reed Block*, DAGA 2021). No measured study of
zinc against aluminium reed plates was found.

## By kind

| Kind | Reed sets | Tremolo (cents at A4) | Construction | Sources |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Bandoneon | two reeds an octave apart (II/II), 142 reeds | dry | long multi-reed plates, zinc (preferred, "large and deep", unmeasured) or Dural-aluminium; hand-ground tongues; wooden case | bando-bando.de ELA 1928, Alfred Arnold pre-1925 |
| Irish | MM (one at pitch, one sharp), LMM, LMMH | dry to wet: modern about 5, older about 27 | B/C or C#/D two-row diatonic, 8-12 basses; Italian tipo a mano or Czech reeds | McNeela's guide; chiffandfipple |
| German | LMMH and more | 14-15 | Hohner, Weltmeister (Klingenthal); club models C/F, 2-3 choirs | Liberty Bellows; dealers |
| French | MMM (authentic musette), 4'+8'+8' (imitation) | modern 18, old 22; asymmetric, about -5/0/+15 | Maugein and Cavagnolo nail or screw plates rather than wax | Wikipedia reed ranks; accordionists.info; La Malle aux Accordéons |
| Italian | LMMH | about 16 today (20 decades ago, 12 preferred by many); one-sided, 0/+20 on three of four boxes measured | a mano reeds on Avional (Duralumin) plates, side gaps to 0.0005 in; wax and rosin; leather valves | Petosa; accordionists.info |
| Balkan | LMMH | dry (Liberty Bellows) or about 15 (the owner's comparison) | button chromatic, double cassotto (Bugari Balkan DM; Scandalli Cromo VI Balkan) | Petosa listing; Thomann |
| Finnish | five-row C system | no published figure; about 19 in the owner's comparison | Italian cassotto boxes (Bugari Finlandia Cassotto) | accordionists.info; FMQ |
| Jazz | 16' in cassotto, dry or swing 8' | under 2 dry, 2-6 swing | cassotto bassoon reeds favoured | Wikipedia reed ranks; accordionists.info |

A cassotto damps the upper harmonics (measured as such by Elejalde-García,
Macho-Stadler, Llanos-Vázquez, *Accordion acoustics: a study on pitch
bending*, Acoustics in Practice 2021): 500-1000 Hz strengthened, above 1 kHz
subdued (Wikipedia, Cassotto, unmeasured). It is usually described with the
16' and the 8' at pitch inside and the tremolo 8' outside.
